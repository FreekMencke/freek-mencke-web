The map as text

  • I push to main. Flux, inside the cluster, deploys the three apps from it: freek-mencke-web, osrs-tracker-web and osrs-tracker-api. MongoDB, Traefik and CrowdSec aren’t Flux’s.
  • Visitors reach the cluster through Traefik, which serves the three apps. CrowdSec sits beside it and bans whoever knocks too hard.
  • osrs-tracker-web talks to osrs-tracker-api, and osrs-tracker-api uses MongoDB.
  • AWS Lambda jobs, outside the cluster, collect player snapshots and counts and send them to a queue, also outside it. osrs-tracker-ingester takes them from the queue and writes them to MongoDB.
  • A few more workloads run on the node and aren’t drawn.
  • Grafana Cloud, outside the cluster, watches the whole thing.
